Day
2
Saturday 28 June
Filitosa + Sartène lunch
Easy half-day — both close to base
South
Deliberately light — you only arrived yesterday. Filitosa is 10 minutes away and takes 90 minutes. Sartène is 25 minutes and has the best wild boar on the island. Back at the villa by mid-afternoon; swim, decompress.
  • 9:30am
    Filitosa Prehistoric Site 6 km · 10 min
    8,000-year-old carved warrior menhirs among millennial olive trees. Allow 60–90 minutes. Ollie will either love it or be bored within 20 minutes — either outcome is fine, it costs nothing to try.
  • 12:30pm
    Lunch in Sartène Should do
    19 km, 25 minutes from base. Order wild boar (sanglier) with chestnut polenta at whichever auberge looks busiest with locals. The town itself — medieval stone alleys, Place de la Libération — is worth 30 minutes of wandering before or after.
    19 km · ~25 min from villa
  • 3:00pm+
    Afternoon free — villa pool
    Back by mid-afternoon. Do nothing. You're on holiday.
Day
3
Sunday 29 June
West Coast — Scandola, Girolata & Calanques de Piana
Long day — the one that earns the drive
West coast Must do
This is the big day. Leave by 7am — the drive to Porto takes nearly 2 hours and the road gets congested. Do the boat first, then Piana on the way home. It's a long day (back around 8–9pm) but everything lands in one trip. Don't attempt this as a leisurely start.
  • 7:00am
    Depart Serra-di-Ferro
    ~90 km north. Head via Ajaccio on the fast road (D81/T10) rather than hugging the coast — faster. Allow 1h 45–2h given early morning.
  • 9:00am
    Scandola Nature Reserve + Girolata boat Must do Pre-book
    Rigid inflatable (Zodiac) from Porto. UNESCO-listed red volcanic cliffs, sea caves, osprey nests. Most tours are 3–4 hours and stop at Girolata — the village accessible only by sea. Ollie will remember this.
    Boat departs Porto marina
  • 1:30pm
    Lunch in Porto or Piana
    Eat near the marina in Porto or drive the 15 minutes south to Piana and eat at A Cantina di l'Orriu (book ahead) — one of the best traditional Corsican restaurants on the island. Worth calling ahead even if you couldn't pre-book.
  • 3:30pm
    Calanques de Piana Must do
    Drive the D81 south from Piana. Stop constantly. The late afternoon light on the red rock is the best time to be here — the early morning crowd has gone and the colours are extraordinary. Walk the short mule track loop if you have energy; 45 minutes, easy terrain.
  • 6:30pm
    Drive home
    ~90 km south. Allow 2 hours. You'll be tired but satisfied. Stop at a roadside bar near Sagone or Tiuccia if you need a break.

Day
5
Tuesday 1 July
Canyoning at Baracci + Ajaccio evening
Active morning · relaxed afternoon · optional evening
Activity Must do
Baracci canyon is 15 km from the villa — your most convenient activity day. Half-day guided canyoning in the morning (the canyon itself takes 2–3 hours). Afternoon free. If you have energy, drive the 43 km to Ajaccio for dinner and a harbour walk — the old town is pleasant in the evening.
  • 8:30am
    Canyoning — Baracci Must do Pre-book
    ~15 km, 20 minutes from villa. Guided half-day: wetsuits and helmets provided, natural water slides, pools, small jumps. No experience needed. Ollie is the perfect age for this. Back by 1pm.
    15 km · ~20 min from villa
  • 1:30pm
    Lunch + villa afternoon
    Back to the villa or lunch in Propriano (11 km away). Afternoon free — pool, read, recover.
  • 6:30pm
    Optional: Ajaccio evening Should do
    43 km, ~50 minutes. Harbour walk, old town, dinner. Napoleon's birthplace is worth a look even without going inside anything. If this feels like too much after canyoning, skip it — you can do Ajaccio on the Saturday market day later in the trip.
    43 km · ~50 min from villa
Second week  ·  2–7 July
Day
6
Wednesday 2 July
Bonifacio — Lavezzi Islands + Citadel sunset dinner
The best day of the trip
South Must do
Pack everything into Bonifacio in one day. Morning boat to Lavezzi, afternoon in the citadel, stay for the sunset, dinner at U Castillé. It's 48 km from the villa — about an hour — so you can leave at a reasonable time. This is the day Ollie will talk about most.
  • 8:00am
    Drive to Bonifacio
    ~48 km, allow 60–70 minutes. Park in the lower marina car park.
    48 km · ~60 min from villa
  • 9:30am
    Lavezzi Islands boat trip Must do Pre-book
    Departs Bonifacio marina. Protected marine reserve — giant granite boulders, crystal water, snorkelling with fearless fish. Bring bread. Most tours are 2.5–3 hours. Back by midday.
  • 1:00pm
    Lunch on the marina, then King of Aragon Staircase Must do
    Eat on the waterfront, then descend the 187 steps carved into the cliff face. Thrilling at sea level with the waves below. Don't underestimate the climb back up.
  • 3:30pm
    Old town & citadel walk Must do
    Wander the medieval alleys of the upper town. Walk the cliff-edge path. Find a spot on the ramparts and wait for the light to change. The limestone turns gold then pink as the sun drops.
  • 8:00pm
    Dinner at U Castillé Pre-book
    In the heart of the old town. Order the aubergines bonifacienne (stuffed with local sheep's cheese) and the wild boar stew. Drive home after — roughly 9:30pm arrival back at villa.

Villa

Day
8
Friday 4 July
Palombaggia + Santa Giulia beach day
The classic Corsican beach day
South Must do
No complicated logistics today. Drive to Palombaggia early, park before the car park fills, spend the morning there. Lunch at Tamaricciu beach shack right next door. Walk or drive 5 minutes south to Santa Giulia for the afternoon — the lagoon is shallower and better for messing around in.
  • 8:30am
    Drive to Palombaggia
    ~47 km via Porto-Vecchio. Leave early — the lower car park (free) fills by 9:30am in late June. Take the D168 south of Porto-Vecchio.
    47 km · ~55 min from villa
  • 9:30am
    Palombaggia Beach Must do
    White sand, umbrella pines, clear water. The postcard beach. Spend the morning here before the afternoon crowds arrive and it starts feeling like a car park.
  • 12:30pm
    Lunch at Tamaricciu Must do
    Right next to Palombaggia. Feet in the sand, shaded canopy, fresh grilled fish, Corsican charcuterie, wood-fired pizza. The Corsican beach lunch in its definitive form. Arrive early or queue.
  • 2:30pm
    Santa Giulia Beach Must do
    5 minutes south of Palombaggia. Shallower, more sheltered lagoon — better for Ollie to explore freely. Paddleboard hire available. Stay until late afternoon, drive home for dinner at the villa.
Day
9
Saturday 5 July
Corte + Restonica Valley
Mountain day — completely different Corsica
Mountains Should do
The furthest day trip from base (~115 km, 1h 45min) but worth it. Corte shows you the island beyond beaches and tourism — fierce, beautiful, independent. The Restonica gorge just outside town has emerald mountain pools that are cooler and wilder than anything on the coast.
  • 7:30am
    Drive to Corte
    ~115 km. Take the N196 north to Ajaccio then the N193 inland. Allow 1h 45min. Bring snacks — there's little on the road through the interior.
    ~115 km · ~1h 45min from villa
  • 9:30am
    Restonica Valley — gorge walk & swimming pools Should do
    Drive the D623 gorge road from Corte (5 km). Park at the end and walk upstream along the river. The pools are cold, clear, and carved into granite. Even a 45-minute walk gets you to spectacular swimming. Go here first while it's cool.
  • 12:30pm
    Corte citadel, belvedere & lunch Should do
    Walk the steep cobblestone streets up to the citadel. The belvedere gives a 360-degree view of the surrounding valleys. Lunch in the town square — there are several decent options on the main drag. Allow 2 hours for lunch + exploration.
  • 3:00pm
    Drive home
    ~115 km south. Back by 5pm. Optional: stop at Ajaccio Saturday market on the way — it runs until noon, so this only works if you leave Corte by 10am and detour through Ajaccio on the way there, not back.
Day
10
Sunday 6 July
Bavella Needles + Cavu pools + Porto-Vecchio evening
Your nearest big landscape — no excuse to skip it
Mountains Must do
Bavella is only 36 km away — the closest major destination on the list. Do it in a half-day, combine with the Cavu natural pools on the way back, then drive into Porto-Vecchio for a proper evening in the old town. Ollie gets gelato; you get charcuterie and a glass of something cold.
  • 8:30am
    Drive to Bavella
    ~36 km via Zonza. Allow 50 minutes — the mountain road is spectacular but slow. Leave early before the car park fills.
    36 km · ~50 min from villa
  • 9:30am
    Bavella Needles — Trou de la Bombe hike Must do
    The hike to the Trou de la Bombe (a massive natural hole blown through the rock) is well-marked, shaded through pine forest, and fully manageable for Ollie. Allow 2–2.5 hours return. The needles as a backdrop are spectacular even from the car park.
  • 12:30pm
    Natural Pools of Cavu Should do
    ~55 km from villa, roughly on the way to Porto-Vecchio. Granite rock slides worn smooth by the river — natural water park. Bring lunch and eat here. Allow 90 minutes.
    ~20 km from Bavella towards Porto-Vecchio
  • 5:30pm
    Porto-Vecchio old town evening Should do
    43 km from villa. Wander the old town at dusk, people-watch from a terrace, order charcuterie and local cheese. Gelato for Ollie. Drive home after dinner — it's your last proper evening out.
    43 km · ~50 min from villa

Villa

Left on the bench

Gorges de Spelunca — would add 45 minutes to the west coast day (Day 3). If you arrive in Porto by 8:30am and the boat isn't until 10am, you could walk the lower section of the gorge from Ota first. Tight but possible.

A Cupulatta Tortoise Sanctuary — sits on the road north from Ajaccio towards Porto. If you left for the west coast day (Day 3) at 6:30am, you could add a 45-minute stop here en route. Ollie's call.

Rondinara Beach — if you have a spare afternoon on the villa days, it's 53 km from base and combines easily with the Lavezzi/Bonifacio area.
