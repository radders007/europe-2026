Beaches
# Experience Type Priority From Serra-di-Ferro Notes
1 Palombaggia Beach Beach Must
~47 km ~55 min
Via Porto-Vecchio. Arrive before 9am — lower car park is free but fills fast. Worth combining with Santa Giulia on the same day.
2 Santa Giulia Beach Must
~48 km ~55 min
Just south of Palombaggia — do both in a single beach day. Shallower and more sheltered; better for Ollie to roam freely. The lagoon behind is good for paddleboarding.
3 Rondinara Beach Should
~53 km ~60 min
Circular bay between Porto-Vecchio and Bonifacio. Near-perfect. Arrive early. Combines well with a Bonifacio half-day.
Boat & Sea
# Experience Type Priority From Serra-di-Ferro Notes
4 Scandola + Girolata Cruise Boat Must
~90 km ~1h 50 min to Porto departure
UNESCO-listed red volcanic cliffs, sea caves, osprey nests. Drive to Porto (or Sagone/Cargèse for a shorter trip) for the boat. Book rigid inflatable (Zodiac) — faster, gets inside the caves. Girolata stop included on most tours. Book weeks ahead. Earns the drive.
5 Lavezzi Islands Boat Must
~67 km ~1h 15 min to Bonifacio marina
Protected marine reserve. Giant granite boulders, snorkelling, Caribbean-clear water. Fish are fearless — bring bread. Book ahead; fills quickly in late June.
Landscapes & Walks
# Experience Type Priority From Serra-di-Ferro Notes
6 Bonifacio — Old Town, Citadel & Sunset Town Must
~48 km ~60 min
The most spectacular town in Corsica. White limestone cliffs, medieval alleys, cliff-edge viewpoints. Stay for the sunset — the light on the limestone is extraordinary. Half-day minimum; pair with Lavezzi boat trip or Rondinara.
7 King of Aragon Staircase Walk Must
~48 km ~60 min
187 steps carved into the cliff face at Bonifacio — do this on the same day as #6. Sea-level at the bottom is thrilling; Ollie will remember it. Remember you have to climb back up.
8 Calanques de Piana Walk Must
~120 km ~2h 10 min to Piana village
UNESCO-listed red porphyry rock towers. Drive it at sunset, stop constantly. Walk the old mule track loop — easy, well-marked, far less congested than the road. Combine with Scandola boat trip on the same west coast day — stay overnight in Porto or Piana to make it worthwhile.
9 Bavella Needles + River Swimming Walk Must
~36 km ~50 min
Closest major landscape to Serra-di-Ferro. Drive the mountain road to the jagged granite spires. Hike to the Trou de la Bombe — manageable for Ollie, great payoff. Swim in the Bavella river pools on the way back.
10 Gorges de Spelunca (Ota) Walk Should
~105 km ~1h 55 min to Ota village
Genoese bridges, deep river pools, pink granite gorge. Easy for children. Park in Ota; walk to the second or third bridge and swim. Combine with Piana and Scandola on a west coast overnight.
11 Restonica Valley (near Corte) Walk Should
~115 km ~1h 45 min approx.
Emerald mountain pools in a granite gorge. Even a short walk is spectacular. Pair with half a day in Corte — citadel and belvedere are worth climbing. Full day trip from base.
12 Corte (mountain capital) Town Should
~110 km ~1h 40 min approx.
The emotional heart of Corsican independence. Completely different feel from the coast. Citadel, belvedere, 360-degree valley views. Pair with Restonica for a full day trip.
13 Natural Pools of Cavu Walk Should
~55 km ~65 min approx.
Natural granite rock slides near Porto-Vecchio. Easy half-day. Good filler on a non-beach day. Combine with Palombaggia/Santa Giulia or Bavella depending on direction.
Activities
# Experience Type Priority From Serra-di-Ferro Notes
14 Family Canyoning (Baracci or Bavella) Activity Must
~15 km ~20 min Baracci (closest)
Baracci canyon is practically on your doorstep — the closest and most family-friendly option. Natural water slides, small jumps, pools. Half-day guided trip; no experience needed. Book ahead. Bavella is also excellent if combining with the needles hike.
15 Filitosa Prehistoric Site Activity Should
~6 km ~10 min
Effectively on your doorstep — 6 km, barely 10 minutes. 8,000-year-old carved warrior menhirs among millennial olive trees. No excuse not to go. Do it on an arrival or departure day.
16 A Cupulatta Tortoise Sanctuary Activity Should
~50 km ~55 min via Ajaccio road
Europe's largest tortoise sanctuary. Works well on the drive north towards Piana/Scandola — it sits on the Ajaccio–Porto road, so you pass near it anyway.
17 Scenic Mountain Train (Ajaccio–Corte) Activity Should
~43 km ~50 min to Ajaccio station
Drive to Ajaccio, board the train. Do the Ajaccio–Corte section or just Ajaccio–Vizzavona for the most dramatic gorge scenery. Good option if the weather turns or you want a low-effort day.
Food & Drink
# Experience Type Priority From Serra-di-Ferro Notes
18 Tamaricciu Beach Shack (Palombaggia) Food Must
~47 km ~55 min
Right next to Palombaggia — do both in a single day. Feet in the sand, fresh grilled fish, Corsican charcuterie, wood-fired pizza under a shaded canopy. The archetypally Corsican beach lunch.
19 Wild boar + polenta at a village auberge Food Must
~19 km ~25 min to Sartène
Sartène is 25 minutes away and the best place on the island for this. Sanglier slow-cooked with maquis herbs, chestnut polenta, house wine. Find one busy with locals. No booking needed — just pick the right-looking place.
20 U Castillé, Bonifacio Citadel Food Should
~48 km ~60 min
Authentic Corsican cooking in the old town. Order the stuffed aubergines (bonifacienne) and wild boar stew. Pair with the Bonifacio sunset day. Book 5–7 days ahead.
21 Evening in Porto-Vecchio Old Town Food Should
~43 km ~50 min
Wander the old town at dusk. Gelato for Ollie, charcuterie and local cheese for you and Sophie, fresh seafood while people-watching. Closest major town to base — easy and repeatable.
22 Ajaccio Saturday Market Food Should
~43 km ~50 min
The best market on the island. Lonzu, coppa, figatellu, brocciu. Buy, find a bench with a view, eat. Saturday mornings only — plan the Ajaccio mountain train day around it if you can.

Book Before You Leave

These will be unavailable if you try to arrange them on the day in late June.
Scandola / Girolata Boat Trip Book 3–4 weeks ahead minimum. Rigid inflatable (Zodiac) from Porto. Tours sell out fast in late June.
Lavezzi Islands Boat Trip Departs Bonifacio marina. High demand — book as soon as you have dates confirmed.
Family Canyoning (Baracci) Guided half-day. Baracci is closest to base. Book through a local operator; groups fill a week or more ahead.
La Bergerie d'Acciola Consistently cited as the island's best mountain dining experience. Book weeks ahead; it is not large.
U Castillé, Bonifacio Popular, small, and inside the old town. Book 5–7 days ahead for an evening slot in late June.
A Cantina di l'Orriu, Piana Best traditional Corsican restaurant on the west coast. Small capacity — call ahead or book online.
From Serra-di-Ferro: You are on the south-west coast — well placed for almost everything in the south. Bonifacio, Lavezzi, Palombaggia, Santa Giulia, and Bavella are all under an hour. Filitosa and Baracci canyoning are under 20 minutes. Ajaccio and Porto-Vecchio are both around 50 minutes. The west coast (Piana, Porto, Scandola) is the one exception — factor in ~2 hours each way and consider an overnight there rather than a brutal there-and-back.
