The trails

Bike Park Bavella has built 28 trails cut into the forest beneath the Aiguilles — Corsica's dramatic granite needle peaks. Everything from wide flowy greens to black-level jump lines with drops. The park has shuttles to take you back up so you can lap all day without pedalling uphill. You bring the energy, the bike does the rest.

🟢 Green

Get the feel of the bike. Wide, flowy, confidence-building.
🔵 Blue

🔴 Red

The plan
Your day,
hour by hour
☀️
08:00
Breakfast at the villa
Big one. You're going to need it. Pack water bottles and snacks the night before.
🚗
08:30
Drive to Zonza
~1 hour through the Alta Rocca mountains. The road itself is ridiculous — hairpins through the maquis, views down to the coast.
🚵
09:30
Gear up + first shuttle
Pick up the bikes, helmets, full-face, pads, gloves. Then first shuttle up to the Col de Bavella. Views at the top are insane.
🔥
10:00 – 13:00
Morning laps
Green warm-up run, then straight onto blue and red. You lap the jump lines. Dad does the cross-country circuit. Whoever finishes first waits at the bottom. Repeat.
🍕
13:00
Lunch in Zonza village
Cold Coke. Pizza. Lie on the grass. Compare runs. Argue about whose crash was worse.
💥
14:00 – 17:00
Afternoon send
One run together on blue or red. Then you push for the black. There's also a guided descent from the Col with a river swim at the bottom — might do this together.
🏆
17:00
Pack up + drive home
Return bikes. Probably too tired to talk. Stop for ice cream on the way back through Propriano.

The shuttle van takes you from Zonza up to the Col. You ride down. At the top: granite needles, pine forest, and views across the whole south of Corsica.

The park is at GPS 41.7506, 9.1692 — Route de Levie, Zonza. It's run by David (builds the trails) and Bérénice (runs the bikes). Every review says the same thing: the trails are brilliant, the setting is unlike anywhere else in Europe.
